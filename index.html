The Story

They'll remember the meal. Make sure they remember you.

Now picture your version. Your logo on the sugar they stir in. Your name on the cutlery they unwrap. Your bottle on the table when they snap a photo for their Story. Nothing loud, nothing forced — just your brand, quietly repeated at every touch, until it's the thing they remember before the food even arrives.

Most owners want exactly this. Few ever chase it. Custom packaging had a reputation for six-figure minimums, confusing print specs, and suppliers who only picked up the phone for big chains. It was never unwanted — just out of reach.

We built BrandMyHoReCa to close that gap. One supplier, six essential products, the lowest MOQ in the market — and a process short enough to go from your logo to your doorstep in a single conversation.
